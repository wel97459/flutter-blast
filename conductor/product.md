# Product Definition: Blockblast Clone

## Initial Concept
A functional mobile game application that clones the popular Blockblast puzzle game, featuring a shelf-based piece placement mechanic on an 8x8 grid, built with Flutter.

## Vision
To create an engaging and visually appealing mobile puzzle game that replicates the core mechanics and strategic depth of Blockblast. The game will provide a smooth, intuitive user experience on mobile platforms, allowing players to strategically place blocks to clear lines and achieve high scores.

## Target Audience
Mobile casual gamers who enjoy puzzle games, brain teasers, and strategy games. Players looking for a challenging yet relaxing experience with simple rules but deep strategic possibilities on their Android or iOS devices.

## Key Features
*   **8x8 Grid Gameplay:** A familiar grid-based puzzle experience where players place blocks.
*   **Shelf-Based Piece Selection:** Players are presented with three unique block pieces per turn, offering strategic choices for placement.
*   **Line Clearing Mechanics:** Clear full rows and columns to score points and make space on the board.
*   **Intuitive Touch Controls:** Drag-and-Drop functionality for seamless piece placement.
*   **Game Over Condition:** Challenging game-over state when no pieces can be placed.
*   **Score Tracking:** Keep track of the player's high scores.
*   **Visual Feedback:** Real-time ghost preview during block dragging for precise placement.

## Future Enhancements (Out of Scope for Initial Release)
*   Multiple game modes (e.g., timed, challenge levels).
*   Power-ups or special blocks.
*   Player profiles and leaderboards.
*   Customizable themes or block designs.
*   Sound effects and background music.
*   Tutorial and onboarding for new players.

## Business Goals
*   Provide an enjoyable and addictive mobile gaming experience.
*   Build a foundation for future mobile game development.
*   Potentially explore monetization options in future iterations (ads, in-app purchases).

## Brand & Tone
*   **Brand:** "Blockblast Mobile" or a similar catchy, descriptive name.
*   **Tone:** Fun, challenging, mentally stimulating, and rewarding. The user experience should be polished and satisfying.
